Amiodarone
Class III Antiarrhythmic  ·  Multi-Class
Action
Prolongs action potential and refractory period. Slows conduction through the AV node. Has Class I, II, III, and IV properties — alpha/beta blocking, sodium/calcium channel effects. Broad antiarrhythmic coverage.
Indications
  • V-Fib / Pulseless V-Tach
  • Stable V-Tach
  • SVT (adenosine-refractory)
  • Rapid AFib / AFlutter
Contraindications
  • Sinus Bradycardia
  • 2nd / 3rd Degree AV Block
  • Cardiogenic Shock
  • Iodine Hypersensitivity
  • Sick Sinus Syndrome
Dose & Route
Cardiac Arrest (V-Fib / Pulseless V-Tach)
Adult: 300 mg IV/IO rapid push — 2nd dose 150 mg
Peds: 5 mg/kg IV/IO rapid push
Stable V-Tach / SVT / Rapid AFib
Adult: 150 mg IV over 10 min, then 1 mg/min infusion
Onset: Immediate IV  ·  Half-life: Weeks
Adverse Effects
  • Hypotension ⚠️
  • Bradycardia
  • Heart Block
  • Prolonged QT
  • Phlebitis (IV site)
  • Nausea
Precautions
Contains Polysorbate 80 — major cause of hypotension, especially with rapid infusion. Check thyroid history (iodine-containing). Monitor BP and ECG continuously.
⚠️ Hypotension Risk
Hypotension is common — have a fluid bolus ready before you push. For stable rhythms, infuse over 10 min — never rapid push unless cardiac arrest.
⚡ Pro Tip
Two very different protocols — arrest vs. stable. In arrest: push it fast. In stable: go slow and watch the BP. Know which situation you're in before you reach for the syringe.
